Mock draft practice and responsible play

Know what your predictions mean.

Free-entry prize contests are planned, not launched. These rules describe the current practice builder and score previews. They are not official rules for a prize competition, an offer of a prize, or a determination of eligibility.

Your practice board

A first-round mock has 32 numbered slots. Each selected player can appear only once. You can identify a team and predict that a pick will be traded, with or without naming the receiving team. A trade prediction is evaluated using the pick's original owner or recorded trade chain and the final selecting team.

A mock saved locally can be edited. A share link contains a snapshot of its predictions. Local saves and shared score previews are not verified, account-bound contest submissions.

Default scoring

Only one score applies to each pick. When multiple normal matches apply, the highest applicable score wins; scores do not stack. For example, a correct position worth 5 takes precedence over a one-pick-away match worth 3.

Trade predictions

Choosing a trade prediction replaces standard scoring for that pick. A named-partner prediction must identify a correct trading team and the final receiving team. A correct player alone does not rescue an incorrect trade call.

Published results and comparisons

Unconfirmed selections remain pending. A potential miss can also remain pending until an adjacent pick is confirmed and the range check can be completed. Paused real results are not exposed through the public score preview. Admin-only historical calibration results do not become public NFL answers.

Public reports compare against published real-result totals for participant and expert entries when available. Equal totals share a rank. The current report is a score comparison, not proof that its owner entered a contest. Other entries' individual picks are not returned by the public comparison endpoint.

These are the default point values. Administrators can configure scoring; a future prize competition must publish its own applicable rules before entries open.

Responsible participation

Treat fantasy preparation and mock drafting as entertainment. Predictions and player values are uncertain; they are not investment advice or a promise of winnings. Take breaks and avoid letting play interfere with personal obligations.

No prize entry, purchase, deposit, or payment is required to use the current practice builder. Do not send money to claim a Hush mock-draft prize based on a practice score or share link.

Before any prize contest opens

A future prize contest requires separately published official rules identifying the operator, eligible ages and locations, entry period, entry limits, prize details, scoring, tie resolution, verification, and award process. Legal review and any required platform approval must happen before launch. AdSense approval would not establish that a contest is lawful or permitted.
