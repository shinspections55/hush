Hushff Fantasy Football

Hush Fantasy Football is a unique drafting experience where teams participate in silent bids on random players each round. Tied bids are settled with our Tied Live Auction at the end of each round. Hush gives you a new draft experience, then lets you export your teams to your normal fantasy football website, including ESPN, Sleeper, and Yahoo.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Hushff?

Hushff is a fantasy football platform where private leagues can organize synchronized silent drafts, manage rosters, review rankings, and run live auctions.

What can I do on Hushff?

You can create or join a private league, participate in a synchronized draft, manage your roster and budget, and review draft results.

How does the silent draft work?

League members join the same draft room, receive the same player pool, submit bids during each round, and see synchronized draft results.
