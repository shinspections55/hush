About Hush

A different way to prepare and draft.

Hush is a fantasy football drafting and preparation website. It brings private bidding, personal player rankings, draft summaries, and first-round NFL mock predictions into one place.

Silent drafts

In a silent draft, league members see a shared group of available players and enter private bids during a timed round. Other managers cannot simply react to each bid as it is entered. The round resolves the bids, and tied high bids can lead to a live tiebreaker auction.

This changes preparation: a manager has to consider several players, roster needs, and the budget still available rather than focusing only on the next name nominated. The draft concept guide explains the format in more detail.

Rankings built around your decisions

My Rankings lets you organize players into tiers, mark favorites, and record your own bid values. On a phone, the Rankings and Player Pool tabs keep the two lists separate. On a desktop, both lists can appear side by side.

Tiers express a useful distinction: two players may be close enough in value that either fills the same need, while the next tier represents a meaningful drop. Personal rankings are preparation tools, not guarantees of future performance.

First-round NFL mock drafts

The mock builder has 32 selection slots. Choose players, identify the selecting teams, and optionally predict a trade. A score preview compares your predictions with confirmed results when those results are published. The report can be printed, downloaded, or shared.

The mock-draft introduction describes the scoring system. The practice rules explain the difference between a local mock, a score preview, and any future prize contest.

What is available now

You can read this page, the draft guide, and the mock-draft information without an account. Some league, account, and draft-room workflows require sign-in. Saved personal rankings and local mock data use the current browser's storage; a shared mock report includes that mock's picks in its link.

The current mock builder is a practice tool. Free-entry prize contests are planned but have not launched. Building or sharing a mock does not submit an entry into a prize contest.
